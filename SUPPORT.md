# Support

## Getting help with the material

This is a volunteer, open-source learning repository. We can help with:

- a broken or incorrect link;
- a confusing explanation;
- a lab that does not work as written;
- a resource that changed its free tier;
- a contribution or pull request;
- a safety or ethical concern about content.

We cannot provide:

- personal tutoring or homework completion;
- help attacking a system, even if you say you have permission;
- legal advice;
- official certification support or exam answers;
- troubleshooting for third-party platforms;
- emergency incident response.

For a real security incident, contact your organization's security team or relevant authorities. Do not use this repository as an incident-response service.

## Before opening an issue

1. Read the relevant guide and its troubleshooting section.
2. Search open and closed issues.
3. Reproduce the problem and capture the exact file, heading, command, error, and date.
4. Remove secrets, personal data, internal hostnames, and client information.
5. Check [FAQ.md](FAQ.md) and [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) guidance.

## Open an issue

Use the repository issue templates for:

- resource suggestions and link updates;
- content corrections;
- bug reports about labs or automation;
- feature and roadmap proposals.

For security-sensitive reports, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

## Contribution help

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Small fixes can go straight to a pull request. For a new roadmap, major section, or translation, open an issue first so we can agree on scope.

## Response expectations

Maintainers review in their spare time. Most issues receive a response within one to two weeks. A polite reminder after 14 days is acceptable. Repeated pings do not speed up volunteer work.

## Community standards

All interactions are governed by the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Harassment, doxxing, unauthorized-access requests, and promotional spam will be removed.
